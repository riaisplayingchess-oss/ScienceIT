BIOTECHNOLOGY

Biotechnology: using biology to solve problems

Biotechnology applies living organisms, cells, or biological molecules to create useful products and processes. People have used it for thousands of years—for example, yeast helps bread rise and microbes help produce yogurt. Modern biotechnology also uses tools such as DNA analysis and cell culture.

  • Health: some medicines, vaccines, and diagnostic tests are made with biological processes.
  • Agriculture: plant breeding and other methods can help develop crops with useful traits.
  • Environment: selected microorganisms can help break down certain pollutants.
🌋
EARTH SCIENCE

Volcanoes: windows into Earth's interior

A volcano is an opening where molten rock, gases, and ash can reach Earth's surface. Molten rock underground is magma; once it erupts onto the surface, it is called lava. Many volcanoes form near moving tectonic plates, although volcanic activity can occur in other settings too.

  • Build-up: magma and gases move through cracks and can collect below the surface.
  • Eruption: pressure and magma properties influence how an eruption behaves.
  • Monitoring: scientists track earthquakes, ground movement, gas, and heat to understand changes.
⚗️
CHEMISTRY

The periodic table: a map of the elements

The periodic table organizes chemical elements by increasing atomic number—the number of protons in an atom's nucleus. Its layout reveals repeating patterns in element properties and helps scientists predict how elements may behave.

  • Rows (periods): elements are arranged from left to right by atomic number.
  • Columns (groups): elements in a group often share similar chemical properties.
  • Element squares: commonly show a symbol, name, atomic number, and atomic mass.
🧬 DNA stores instructions

DNA carries biological information in cells. Genes are sections of DNA that contribute to how an organism develops and functions.

🌍 Earth is always changing

Volcanism, erosion, earthquakes, and the movement of tectonic plates are among the processes that shape Earth's surface.

🔬 Elements build matter

Everything made of ordinary matter is composed of atoms. Each element is defined by the number of protons in its atoms.
