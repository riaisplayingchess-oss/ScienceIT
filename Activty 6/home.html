About

A place to explore science.

Science IT is a web app created to help students explore science in a clear, approachable way. It offers learning content about chemistry and other science topics. The site encourages students to ask questions and keep learning.

Who are we?

Made for science lovers.

We are science lovers who enjoy discovering how the world works. We believe curiosity can lead to meaningful questions and new learning. Science IT brings science topics together for everyone who wants to explore.
